Profile

Where accounting discipline meets systems thinking

A finance professional who doesn't just close the books — he questions why the process takes as long as it does, and builds the fix.

Kishore's work sits at the intersection of accounting, compliance, and technology. As Accounts Manager at BigFix Gadget Care LLP, he leads the Accounts and Operations teams, finalizes financial reports for audit, and coordinates GST input reconciliation to protect the company's input tax credit — the traditional core of the role.

What sets his approach apart is what he did next: rather than accepting repetitive manual accounting and claims-tracking as fixed overhead, he initiated and led the design of an automated accounting and claims management system, working directly with the IT team to convert operational bottlenecks into a centralized digital platform — detailed in the featured project below.

He works primarily in Tally Prime / ERP 9 for day-to-day accounting, with Excel for reconciliation, reporting, and data organization, and describes his approach simply: identify where a process is leaking time or accuracy, map it clearly, and coordinate with whoever owns the next step until it's fixed.
